# UX patterns observed on Tracend and how they map to Meridian

Tracend's core promise: it turns data into today's decision. Instead of
showing a score alone, it shows the score, the decision, and the reasons.

| # | Pattern on Tracend | What it means | Meridian version |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One score plus one action ("Low. Go lighter today.") | Never show a number without a next step | **Today card**: Day score + "Today's call" |
| 2 | Reasons shown with every adjustment | Evidence builds trust | Every call lists 2-3 **evidence chips** that open the source screen |
| 3 | "Rewind to Monday evening" story | Explain cause and effect in time order | **Why this score?** timeline of yesterday's events |
| 4 | Sliders: change the night, watch the morning change | Let the user test outcomes | **What-if simulator** on Cash flow (and a budget one) |
| 5 | Every reading vs "your own normal" | Personal baselines, not averages | **Rolling personal normal** for spend, sleep, habits |
| 6 | Five apps in one that sees the whole day | Cross-domain insight | Insights that join sleep, spend, tasks, bills |
| 7 | Coach answers with your numbers, shows evidence and what it could not see | Honest AI | **Ask Meridian** with evidence and a "could not see" list |
| 8 | Nothing changes without your OK; each approved plan saved as a version | User stays in control | **Proposals**: budget and goal changes need approval, saved as versions |
| 9 | Plan keeps working if AI or network is down | Core logic is not AI | **Rule-based engine** works offline; AI is optional |
| 10 | Weekly review calculated from logs, no AI | Deterministic where possible | Weekly review (already designed) stays rule-based |
| 11 | Muscle map: what the week covers and skips | Show gaps, not just totals | **Coverage map** of the week (bills, goals, habits, meals) |
| 12 | Trends over months, not daily noise | Smooth data | 7-day averages and monthly trend lines |
| 13 | 3-step onboarding: goal, it learns you, you approve | Short, honest setup | **Money goal setup** in 3 steps |
| 14 | Privacy panel: AI off, export, delete; consent names the AI company | Trust | **Privacy and AI** screen and consent sheet |
| 15 | Plain, direct copy | Short sentences, verbs | Voice rules in the spec |

## Do not copy
- Tracend's text, brand name, logo, illustrations, example athletes or
  screenshots. Take patterns only. Write original copy.
- Medical or training claims. Meridian is a money and life tracker.

## Keep from Meridian
- Palette, components, motion rules and all existing screens.
- Tab bar: Home, Finance, Garage, Tasks, Wealth. "Today" becomes the top
  of Home, not a new tab.
